Training app for League of Legends players

Practice team decisions while you wait in queue.

This is a small mobile app with short training games. It helps players below Diamond learn to think as a team: what their comp needs in draft, and what the right move is in a given moment of a game.

Two training games

The player picks their main role once. Both games then adapt to that role. Mock-ups below use champion types instead of names.

Game 1

Draft

The player sees four allies and five enemies and picks the champion that fits their team best. It is about team composition, not individual counterpicks. Players can save their own champion pool, so the options come from champions they actually play.

Game 2

Decision making

The player sees a moment from a game drawn as a simple map: who is alive, where the waves are, what objective is up. They choose the best next move out of four and get a short explanation.

18:40 Your team won a fight. 3 enemies are dead for the next 30 seconds. The objective in the lower river is up.
Start the objective now
Everyone recalls to buy items
Split up and farm side lanes
Chase the last two enemies

5 v 2 for 30 seconds and the objective is up. The enemies who could contest it are dead.

How the right answer is decided

Every answer comes from a fixed set of rules, so it can always be explained. The explanation shows the two rules that mattered most, with the numbers from the situation. If two answers score too close to each other, the situation is not used. A Masters player reviews samples every week.

frontline < 4The team needs someone who can stand in front in a fight.
magic damage < 20%One damage type is easy to itemize against.
alive 5 v 2A numbers advantage should be turned into an objective.
enemy engage ≥ 7Peel and disengage count double.

Commitments

  • Free for everyone. No paid features and no exclusive access.
  • No Riot logos, and no design that imitates the League client.
  • Never presented as approved by or partnered with Riot Games.
  • Real matches are anonymous. No player is named, rated or shamed.
  • Levels in the app measure training progress, not League rank. No MMR or rank estimates.
  • Works outside the game. No overlay, no live game data, no in-game advantage.
  • Development key only for this prototype. Public release only with a production key.
  • Only documented endpoints. No scraping.
